1. Passenger Master ListApne sabhi yatri ek baar save karo - naam (ID ke anusaar), umar, gender, berth, khana. Baar-baar kaam khatam.
2. Train searchFrom/To station chuno (13,000+ stations) aur date. Results par sort aur class filter laga sakte ho.
